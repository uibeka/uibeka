## Uchenna Ibeka

Founder of **AsymmetrIQ Labs**, a quantitative research laboratory working at the
intersection of machine learning and financial markets. I lead the lab's research
direction and its work on T.I.T.A.N., an autonomous decision-making system
currently in development.

Before AsymmetrIQ Labs I worked in payroll and people operations in the digital
asset industry - multi-jurisdiction payroll, equity administration, and the
controls that keep both auditable. That work left me convinced that a system
nobody can inspect, constrain, or explain isn't a finished system, however well
it behaves on a good day. It's the idea behind the governance-layer architecture
the lab builds around.

I hold an M.A. in Labor Studies and Employment Relations from Rutgers University,
and I contribute to [Automaton](https://github.com/Conway-Research/automaton), the
open-source autonomous-agent project from Conway Research. My merged work there has
centred on the agent discovery layer: the on-chain registry lookups that let
independent agents find one another.
